🚨 How to report a scam in India
  1. Lost money? Call 1930 immediately. The first hour matters most.
  2. File a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in with screenshots, the scammer's number and the transaction ID.
  3. Tell your bank or UPI app right away so it can try to freeze the payment.
  4. Report suspicious calls, SMS and WhatsApp numbers on Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu).
  5. In WhatsApp, open the chat, tap the number, then Report and Block.
  6. Keep the message until your complaint is filed. Never share your OTP, PIN or password.

🔒 Privacy and how we use your data
  • Reports are anonymous. The map and live feed keep only the district, the verdict, the scam type and a short snippet. Phone numbers, long digit runs (OTP, card, account) and UPI IDs are removed from the snippet first.
  • Golden Hour details stay in your browser. The name, amount, transaction ID and account digits you type there are used only to prepare your letters. They are not sent to our server.
  • Family guardian. If you add a guardian on WhatsApp, we store the two phone numbers only to send alerts. You can remove it any time by sending STOP.
  • Data for police and banks is a summary of anonymous trends and linked scam identifiers, not personal details of the people who checked messages.
  • Feedback. The "Was this right?" buttons store only the verdict and a yes or no.
  • Not legal or financial advice. SurakshaSathi gives an automated opinion and can be wrong. When in doubt, do not pay, do not share an OTP or PIN, and call your bank or 1930.
